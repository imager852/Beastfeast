Our story 关于我们

A canteen for big appetites

Beast Feast Canteen 饗獸食堂 is a Hong Kong-style cha chaan teng 茶餐廳 on Chapel Street, Bristol, just two minutes' walk from Unite Students. We serve Hong Kong café classics alongside favourites from Taiwan, for big appetites: rice boxes, noodles, street-food tapas and desserts.

Every dish is cooked fresh to order, in generous portions. Collect from us for the best value and our exclusive meal deals, or get it delivered across Bristol with Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Just Eat or HungryPanda.
